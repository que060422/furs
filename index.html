GOOD QUESTIONS, CARING HEARTS

Things you might be wondering.

Thinking about adopting or helping out? Here are some answers to get you started.

💬
Still have questions? Talk to us →
What do I need to adopt a pet?

Requirements depend on the rescue organization. You may be asked about your living situation, ability to provide care, and plans for keeping the animal safe. Contact the organization for its actual requirements.

Is there an adoption fee?

Some organizations charge an adoption fee to help cover veterinary care, vaccinations, sterilization, or other expenses. Ask the organization about its fees and what they include.

Will my home need to be checked?

Some rescues conduct home visits or virtual checks, while others use different screening procedures. Confirm the process with the organization handling the adoption.

Can I foster instead of adopting?

Fostering can be a way to provide temporary care while an animal waits for a permanent home. Availability, duration, and responsibilities depend on the rescue program.

How can I volunteer?

You can ask about opportunities such as animal care, events, transport, community outreach, and supply drives. The organization can explain its current needs and requirements.

What items can I donate?

Possible items include pet food, blankets, towels, cleaning supplies, carriers, and other animal-care essentials. Check with the rescue first to confirm what is needed and what it can accept.

What should I do if I find an injured animal?

Keep yourself safe, avoid handling an injured animal unless necessary, and contact a local veterinarian, animal welfare group, or appropriate local authority for guidance.
